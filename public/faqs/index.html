Frequently asked questions

Have a question that isn't answered here? Get in touch.

How do I get started?

You can reach me through my contact form or email me.

What services do you offer?
  • Individual therapy
  • Couples therapy
  • Premarital counseling and assessment
  • Family therapy
How long are appointments?

Appointments are 50 minutes with some evening appointments available.

Do you take health insurance?

Clear Step Counseling is not a network provider for any insurance plans so your appointments will be considered out of network. I can complete forms provided and required by your insurance company and can provide you with a detailed statement for claim reimbursement. However, you are ultimately responsible for full payment of fees.

What is your rate?

Please contact clearstep@counselingmail.com for rates.

What methods of payment do you accept?

Cash, check and credit card are accepted for payment.
