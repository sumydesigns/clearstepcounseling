Privacy Policy

Clear Step Counseling, LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

Information you provide

When you submit the contact form, we receive the name, email address, phone number and message you enter. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry. Form submissions are delivered by email through Web3Forms, a third-party form processing service.

Submitting the contact form does not establish a therapist-client relationship. Please do not include sensitive health information in website messages.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) for security and performance. This website does not sell or share your personal information.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to external sites such as Google Maps and LinkedIn, which have their own privacy policies.

Questions
