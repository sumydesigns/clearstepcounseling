About

Dr. Cezanne Elias

Marriage & Family Therapist, PhD, LMFT

Dr. Cezanne Elias is a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist, and a clinical member of the American Association for Marriage and Family Therapy. She received her PhD in Marriage and Family Therapy from Purdue University. In addition to her private practice, Dr. Elias is an Associate Clinical Professor at Purdue University.

She is the recipient of numerous awards, has authored several publications, and has presented on her research, teaching, and clinical work at local and national conferences.

Choosing the right therapist is one of the most important first steps. I look forward to answering any questions you might have about my practice and to helping you assess if I am the right therapist for you.
